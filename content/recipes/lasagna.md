<h2>Recipe Info</h2>
<div><b>Servings:</b>12</div>

<b>you need a 9x13 casserole dish to make this</b> so either get one or get one of those single use foil ones from the grocery store

<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul>
  <li>450g ground beef</li>
  <li>4 yellow onions (white, vidalia, etc. also ok)</li>
  <li>1 head roast garlic (or 4 cloves minced garlic)</li>
  <li>1/4 cup red wine</li>
  <li>some kind of beef flavouring. i use 1 tsp beef better than bouillon paste. beef bouillon powder or a cube could also work</li>
  <li>~680mL marinara sauce</li>
  <li>1 tsp salt</li>
  <li>pinch sugar (idk like 1/2 tsp)</li>
  <li>1 tsp dried thyme</li>
  <li>4 tbsp fresh parsley</li>
  <li>9 lasagna noodles</li>
  <li>450g cottage cheese</li>
  <li>425g ricotta cheese</li>
  <li>1 egg</li>
  <li>450g mozzarella cheese</li>
</ul>

<h2>Instructions</h2>
<b>Preparing Ingredients:</b>
<ol>
  <li>mince the parsley and set aside</li>
  <li>dice the onion and set aside</li>
  <li>mince the garlic (if not using roast garlic) and set aside</li>
  <li>grate the mozzarella and set aside</li>
  <li>cook the 9 lasagna noodles to al dente (package usually has a time for this,) drain, and set aside. put in a little cold water to prevent them from sticking</li>            
</ol>
<b>Meat Sauce:</b>
<ol>
  <li>heat a pot over medium heat and add 1 tbsp of oil</li>
  <li>add the onions to the pot and saute until browned. if the onions are sticking, add butter and/or deglaze the pot with some red wine</li>
  <li>add the beef to the pot and continue cooking until the beef is no longer pink (~5 min)</li>
  <li>add the garlic. if using minced garlic, saute until it's fragrant (~1 min)</li>
  <li>add 1/4 cup red wine and the beef flavouring. keep cooking until the wine is mostly evaporated</li>
  <li>add the marinara (~680mL), 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp dried thyme, a pinch of sugar, and 2 tbsp parsley. bring the mixture up to a simmer, cover with a lid and cook for 5 minutes. then set aside</li>            
</ol>
<b>Cheese Sauce:</b>
<ol>
  <li>combine the following in a large mixing bowl and set aside:</li>
  <ul>
    <li>450g cottage cheese</li>
    <li>425g ricotta cheese</li>
    <li>1 egg</li>
    <li>2 tbsp parsley</li>
    <li>~115g grated mozzarella cheese (about 1 cup)</li>
  </ul>             
</ol>
<b>Assembly:</b>
<ol>
  <li>preheat oven to 375˚f</li>
  <li>get out the 9x13 casserole dish and spread 1/2 cup of the meat sauce on the bottom (of the inside. not the outside that would be bad)</li>
  <li>repeat 3 times:</li>
    <ol>
    <li>lay down 3 lasagna noodles</li>
    <li>spread on 1/3rd of the meat sauce</li>
    <li>sprinkle 1 cup mozzarella cheese on top</li>
    <li>spread 1/2 of the cheese sauce on top</li>
    </ol>
  <li>poke 12 toothpicks into the lasagana (evenly spaced from eachother) and cover the lasagna with a piece of aluminium foil.</li>    
  <li>put the lasgana in the oven and bake for 45 minutes</li>
  <li>let the lasagna rest for 30 minutes before eating. or don't if you're really hungry but it will slopify if you don't let it rest</li>        
</ol>
