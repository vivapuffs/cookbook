<h2>Recipe Info</h2>
<div><b>Servings:</b> 6? maybe 8</div>

<h2>Ingredients</h2>
<ul>
  <li>4 cups chicken stock</li>
  <li>3 tbsp tomato paste</li>
  <li>1 tbsp fish sauce</li>
  <li>30g unflavoured gelatin (see notes)</li>
  <li>1 tbsp soy sauce</li>
  <li>1 tbsp worcestershire sauce</li>
  <li>4 tbsp vegetable oil</li>
  <li>1kg chuck roast beef</li>
  <li>2 packages brown bella mushrooms</li>
  <li>4 medium carrots</li>
  <li>4 ribs celery</li>
  <li>6 yellow onions (white, vidalia, etc. are ok too)</li>
  <li>1 head roast garlic (or 4 minced garlic cloves)</li>
  <li>red wine</li>
  <li>2 tbsp flour</li>
  <li>2 bay leaves</li>
  <li>4 sprigs thyme (or about 1 tbsp dried thyme)</li>
  <li>500g white potatoes</li>
  <li>2 tbsp parsley</li>                           
</ul>

<h2>Instructions</h2>
<ol>
  <li>quarter half of the mushrooms. thinly slice the rest. set aside</li>
  <li>dice the onions and set aside</li>
  <li>slice the carrots into discs (or if they are really girthy, quarter first and slice into cubes) and set aside</li>
  <li>dice the celery and set aside</li>
  <li>mince the garlic (if you aren't using a head of roast garlic) and set aside</li>
  <li>mince the parsley and set aside</li>
  <li>dice the potatoes (aim for bite size pieces, bigger chunks won't cook well due to the amount of tomato paste in this recipe) and put them in a container where you can cover them with water. cover with water and set aside</li>
  <li>wow that was a lot of fucking cutting vegetables. now preheat the oven to 300˚f :)</li>
  <li>in a large pot (must be oven safe, think dutch oven) heat 2tbsp of oil on medium high until shimmering</li>
  <li>take out your chuck roast and rub it with salt and pepper</li>
  <li>sear each side of the chuck roast, each side will take about 5 minutes, then set aside</li>
  <li>add all of the mushrooms as well as half of the onions, carrots, and celery to the pot the meat was in. cook until the vegetables are browned. use some red wine to deglaze the bottom of the pot as needed</li>
  <li>while the vegetables are browning, cut the chuck roast into bite size pieces (about an inch or so), then transfer to a large bowl and toss with flour</li>
  <li>once the vegetables have browned to your liking, add the garlic and cook for an extra minute or two.</li>
  <li>once the garlic is fragrant, add the beef into the pot as well as:</li>
   <ul>
    <li>4 cups of chicken stock</li>
    <li>30g unflavoured gelatin</li>
    <li>1 tbsp soy sauce</li>
    <li>1 tbsp worcestershire sauce</li>
    <li>3 tbsp tomato paste</li>
    <li>1 tbsp fish sauce</li>
    <li>2 bay leaves</li>
    <li>1 tbsp dried thyme (or 4 sprigs fresh thyme)</li>
    <li>2 tbsp parsley</li>
   </ul>
  <li>bring the mixture to a simmer. then cover the pot partially with a lid (like 75% of the pot, shouldn't be fully covered) and put it in the oven for 1 and a half hours</li>
  <li>while the pot is in the oven, heat a pan over medium heat with 2 tbsp of oil</li>
  <li>add the remaining onions to the pan and stir occassionally until browned. use the red wine to deglaze as needed</li>
  <li>once the onions are browned, add the remaining carrots and celery to the pan and cook until the celery softens</li>
  <li>once the stew has finished its first round in the oven, take it out and add the potatoes and the vegetbales you fried in the pan</li>
  <li>then put the pot back in the oven for an hour</li>
  <li>check the potatoes and beef after the stew has been in the oven for the second time. if they are to your liking, serve the soup, if not, put it in for another 30 minutes and it should be good</li>
</ol>

<h2>Notes</h2>
unflavoured gelatin. what the heck? i didn't believe in it but it gives the broth a nice consistency. you can do without but the broth will be thin

this goes great with a nice sourdough. you can also add some frozen peas right at the end if you fuck with those but sadly my boyfriend does not so i Don't Do That
